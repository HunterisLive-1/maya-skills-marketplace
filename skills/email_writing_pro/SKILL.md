# Email Writing Pro

Jab Boss koi email likhne/draft karne ko bole, ye rules follow karo:

## Structure (hamesha)
1. **Subject line**: 6-10 words, specific, action-oriented. Kabhi "Hello" ya "Important" jaisa vague nahi.
2. **Greeting**: "Dear <Name>," professional ke liye; "Hi <Name>," known contacts ke liye.
3. **Opening line**: Seedha purpose — "I am writing to..." / "Following up on...". Koi "I hope this email finds you well" filler NAHI (sirf tab jab Boss bole formal-warm chahiye).
4. **Body**: 2-3 short paragraphs max. Ek paragraph = ek idea. Bullets use karo jab 3+ items hon.
5. **Call to action**: Clearly bolo kya chahiye aur kab tak — "Could you confirm by Friday, 12 July?"
6. **Sign-off**: "Best regards," + Boss ka naam (SIGNATURE_NAME env se, warna Boss se pucho).

## Tone rules
- Default: professional but warm. Corporate jargon avoid karo ("synergy", "circle back").
- Complaint/escalation email: firm but polite, facts + dates ke saath, blame language nahi.
- Follow-up email: pehli line mein previous email ka reference + date.
- Apology email: ownership lo, excuse mat do, concrete fix batao.

## Language
- Output email hamesha ENGLISH mein (jab tak Boss Hindi email na maange).
- Numbers, dates, amounts exact likho — "next week" ki jagah "by Monday, 14 July".
- Email 150 words se chhota rakho jab tak content zyada na demand kare.

## Kya NAHI karna
- Draft bina Boss ko dikhaye send nahi karna (email_send tool tabhi jab Boss confirm kare).
- Recipient ka naam pata na ho to "Dear Sir/Madam" ki jagah Boss se pucho.
