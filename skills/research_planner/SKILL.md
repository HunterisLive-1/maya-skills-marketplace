# Research & Plan Writer

Jab Boss bole "research karo", "plan banao", "strategy likho", "X ke baare mein pata karo aur batao kya karna chahiye" — ye method follow karo.

## Step 1 — Scope lock karo (30 seconds)
Ek hi baar mein confirm karo: exact goal kya hai, deadline/budget hai kya, aur output kis form mein chahiye (bola hua summary, document, ya PDF). Agar Boss ne already bata diya hai to mat pucho — seedha kaam shuru.

## Step 2 — Research (web se latest info nikaalo)
- Kam se kam 3 alag angles se search karo: (1) overview/basics, (2) latest news/trends is saal ki, (3) problems/risks/criticism.
- Har claim ke saath source ka naam yaad rakho — plan mein "kahan se pata chala" bolna credibility deta hai.
- Numbers collect karo: prices, dates, percentages, market size. Vague research bekaar hai.
- Conflicting info mile to dono side note karo aur batao kaunsa zyada reliable lagta hai.

## Step 3 — Plan structure (hamesha yahi format)
```
1. GOAL — ek line, measurable (kya + kab tak)
2. CURRENT SITUATION — research se 3-4 key findings (numbers ke saath)
3. ACTION PLAN — phases mein:
   Phase 1 (abhi se X din): concrete steps, har step ka owner/resource
   Phase 2, Phase 3...
4. BUDGET/RESOURCES — kya chahiye, approx cost
5. RISKS — top 3 risks + har ek ka mitigation
6. NEXT STEP — aaj/kal ka SABSE pehla concrete kaam
```

## Step 4 — Delivery
- Pehle 30-second verbal summary bolo (goal + 3 key points + first step).
- Phir pucho: "Poora plan document/PDF mein chahiye?" — haan bole to document bana do.
- Plan mein har recommendation ke peeche research wala reason ho — "kyunki X% log...", "kyunki iski cost Y hai".

## Kya NAHI karna
- Research skip karke general gyaan se plan mat banao — har plan mein kam se kam 3 researched facts hone chahiye.
- 10 page ka essay mat likho — plan actionable hona chahiye, decoration nahi.
- Saare options list karke Boss par decision mat chhodo — ek clear recommendation do, alternatives ek line mein.
- Fake numbers/sources kabhi mat banao — data nahi mila to seedha bolo "is par reliable data nahi mila".
